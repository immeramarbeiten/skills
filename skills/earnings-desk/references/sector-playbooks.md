# Sector playbooks: choose metrics by business economics

## SaaS / enterprise software (e.g. NOW)
Subscription revenue, organic/constant-currency growth, cRPO/RPO, net retention (if reported), large-customer growth, ACV, gross retention, subscription gross margin, operating margin GAAP vs non-GAAP, SBC and dilution, FCF margin, billings/deferred revenue seasonality. Identify whether AI revenue metrics are consumption, booked ACV or recognized revenue — never equate them. Relevant peers based on go-to-market and scale, not automatic broad software average.

## Search, advertising, cloud platforms (e.g. GOOGL)
Search/ads revenue, distribution and TAC, YouTube or other ad supply/demand, cloud growth and *incremental* margin, capital intensity and AI infrastructure returns, capex commitments, depreciation, energy/network constraints, cash return and one-time investment mark-to-market effects. Separate platform operating trends from equity-security gains/losses. Peers can vary by segment: ads vs ads, cloud vs cloud, not a single total-company comp.

## Semiconductors and AI hardware
End-market units/pricing, revenue vs shipments, mix, foundry/network constraints, gross margins, customer concentration, inventories, lead times, backlog quality, capex, cycle-normalized earnings and share of software/ecosystem value.

## Consumer/retail/e-commerce
Comparable sales, transactions vs ticket, units, gross margin and promotions, return rates, customer acquisition/retention, inventory turns, labor/occupancy leverage, cash conversion, geographic/channel mix, and seasonality. Rule out inventory build driving revenue illusions.

## Banks, asset managers, insurers
Banks: NII, NIM, fee income, credit quality, charge-offs, NPLs, CET1, liquidity, deposits/funding, loan mix, cost/income and duration. Insurers: combined ratio, underlying underwriting result, reserve development, solvency capital and investments. Do not use SaaS FCF or net debt ratio as primary financial health scores for a deposit-taking bank.

## Healthcare, biotech, pre-revenue
Drug/program milestones and clinical readouts, cash runway, trial design, FDA/EMA interactions, licensing/partnership milestones, burn rate, dilution and contingent liabilities. Pre-revenue biotech may receive N/R operating profitability scoring; do not imply negative earnings automatically indicate poor execution. Confirm regulatory and trial status from original sources.

## Industrials / utilities / autos
Order intake and backlog, pricing vs volume, industrial margin, working capital, cash conversion, net leverage, debt maturities, maintenance vs growth capex, asset utilization, regulatory exposure and cyclical mid-cycle comparisons.

## Special cases
Conglomerates: grade total company but add segment health exceptions. Newly IPO'd companies: comparisons may be sparse, flag low confidence. Acquisition-driven growth: distinguish reported vs organic. Highly cyclical producers: normalize through cycle. Negative denominators: never present meaningless percentage surprises (e.g. moving from a negative estimate to positive FCF) without careful wording.
